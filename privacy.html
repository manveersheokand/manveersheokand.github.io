Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Channel Pipeline ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (Think Quick Club and Parallel Plane).

What the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: uploading videos to YouTube (scope youtube.upload). It uses this only to upload videos the app has created to the owner's channels.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read, collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone.
  • It does not access viewers' accounts, comments, watch history or analytics.
  • It is not offered to other users.

How permission is stored

The sign-in permission (a refresh token) is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated upload job.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
