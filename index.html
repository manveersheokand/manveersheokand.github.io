Channel Pipeline

A private tool that makes and uploads videos for my own YouTube channels.

Channel Pipeline is a small personal project. Each day it creates short educational and puzzle videos and uploads them to my own channels:

  • Think Quick Club (@ThinkQuickClub): riddles and "Would You Rather" questions
  • Parallel Plane (@ParallelPlaneTV): "what if" scenarios explained with real science

The app only accesses YouTube to upload videos to these channels, which I own. It is not offered to other users.
